I design and analyse discrete choice experiments (DCE), best-worst scaling, and contingent valuation studies to quantify how patients, the public, and policymakers value health and healthcare. A recurring theme is **health inequality aversion** — how much society is willing to trade efficiency for equity — and how it varies geographically.

Applications span targeted cancer therapies, rare-disease drug reimbursement, blood-donation incentives, long-term care insurance, and IVF, published in *PharmacoEconomics*, *The Patient*, and *Value in Health*.
