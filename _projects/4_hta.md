Alongside applied evaluations, I develop methods that make economic evaluation fit for equity-informed decision-making: aggregate DCEA for resource allocation, incorporating productivity loss and future unrelated medical costs, ICER threshold adjustment for equity considerations, and cost-effectiveness thresholds based on the value of a statistical life.

This methodological work supports HTA processes in Australia (PBAC/MSAC) and internationally, and is published in *Global Health Research and Policy*, *BMJ Global Health*, and the *European Journal of Health Economics*.
