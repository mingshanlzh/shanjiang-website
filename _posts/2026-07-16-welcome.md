Welcome, and thanks for visiting. I rebuilt this site to bring my research, publications, and talks together in one place — and to have somewhere to write.

I plan to use this blog for short, practical pieces: notes on **distributional cost-effectiveness analysis (DCEA)**, reflections from conferences and research visits, annotated **R** code for health economic models, and the occasional thought on equity in health policy.
